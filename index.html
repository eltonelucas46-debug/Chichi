The setting

Maseno University

24 August 2026. I had joined the Bachelor of Pharmacy programme with IT. I was focused on beginning university life, completely unaware that the admission queue had something else in store for me.

👑

The unexpected queen

A beautiful light-skinned girl was standing behind me, waiting for her turn. The admission lady called her name: Felista Mutuku. I noticed her beauty, and just like that, she stayed on my mind.

The chapters of us

How a stranger became my favourite plot twist.

Every little moment brought us here. Some were sweet, some were funny, and some were absolutely embarrassing. 😂

24 August 2026 · The first sight

The admission queue 👑

I saw a beautiful girl behind me at the BPharm admission desk. I heard her name and immediately had something to tell my friends: I'd spotted a queen. I didn't know her yet, but she had already caught my attention.

The beginning of everything

First week · Orientation

Eyes on the queen ✨

During the first week of university orientation, my eyes kept finding her. While learning my way around university life, I was also quietly admiring the beauty that had caught my attention in the admission queue.

Secret admiration

Second week · The plot twist

Blocked before the story began 😂

I finally got her number and texted her on WhatsApp. Unfortunately, my first approach involved vulgar language, and she blocked me. Romance? Not quite. A lesson in communication? Absolutely.

I stayed blocked until the following Thursday, when fate—or perhaps the Maths timetable—put me in the seat beside her.

The WhatsApp incident

The following Thursday · Maths class

My second chance 💌

After the short Maths lesson, I gathered enough courage to ask why she'd blocked me. She explained herself and unblocked me. We started chatting again, although not very much at first.

And because I feared being blocked again, I mostly texted when I needed help with assignments. Yes, assignments. 😂

A very academic love strategy

Third week · Another Thursday

A conversation that mattered 🌷

I spoke with her again while my friends were around. I didn't realise that this was also when she started becoming interested in me. I was trying not to bother her too much, so she became more familiar with my friends than with me.

Feelings quietly growing

Fourth week · Saturday

The church invitation ⛪

She spent the day visiting my friend and later passed by my room to greet us. Then she invited me to church the following day. That night, we texted about church and preparing early.

The next morning, we met and went to church together. Afterwards, she visited me in my room, with several friends around. Nothing particularly special happened—but our friendship felt easier afterwards.

Faith brought us closer

The following weekend · Plans changed

Pharmacy, messages & chemistry 🧪

I tried to arrange another meeting, but she was going to World Pharmacists Day at Mount Kenya University. We kept chatting, and our conversations became more playful and personal.

Then came Monday, when she invited me to visit her hostel. We spent time together, and our connection grew beyond the ordinary friendship we'd started with.

The chemistry was changing

That Monday, Wednesday & beyond

When friendship became something more ❤️

Our visits continued, we grew more comfortable around each other, and our closeness deepened. By Wednesday, we'd shared another meaningful moment together. Thursday brought another visit, followed by a Friday sleepover that lasted until Saturday morning.

And now there's one question I still can't answer with certainty: did we start dating on Monday or Wednesday? 😂

The Monday-or-Wednesday mystery

Present day · Still unfolding

The story is not finished 🌹

From one admission queue to shared conversations, church, laughter, visits and growing affection—this is the story so far. The best part is that we don't have to know every chapter in advance to keep writing it.

The letter at the end of the universe

To Felista, my unexpected queen.

If someone had told me on 24 August 2026 that a girl standing behind me in an admission queue would become part of such a memorable story, I probably would have laughed.

I didn't have a perfect beginning. I made mistakes, got blocked, found courage in a Maths lesson, and sometimes used assignments as an excuse to talk to you. 😂 But somehow, our story kept moving forward.

I treasure the moments that brought us closer, the laughter along the way, and the memories still waiting to be made. Whatever the next chapter holds, I'm glad our paths crossed at Maseno.

And as for whether it was Monday or Wednesday when our story became something more… I suppose that mystery deserves its own anniversary debate. 😜

Thank you for becoming part of my story, Chichi. May the chapters ahead bring us kindness, understanding, laughter and beautiful memories.

With affection, from the boy in the admission queue. ♡
